MYGC Midyear Activity 2025

May 27, 2025Mactan Bank

MYGC successfully held its 2025 Mid-Year Activity last May 4, 2025, focusing on promoting mental health in the workplace through engaging wellness initiatives. The event featured a special talk on mental health awareness, highlighting the importance of being mentally healthy and resilient. From guided meditation sessions to interactive workshops, employees enjoyed a day dedicated to well-being and self-care—reinforcing MYGC’s commitment to a supportive and healthy work environment.

To foster engagement across the branches, Mactanbank was organized into four clusters: Cebu North, Cebu South, Leyte area, and the Negros Island Region. One of the main highlights of the day was the awarding of the Top Performing Branch and Employee of the Year, recognizing outstanding performance and dedication within the organization.
