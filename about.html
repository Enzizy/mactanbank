Banking that stays close to the community.

Corporate profile

One of the largest rural banks in the Visayas.

Mactan Rural Bank (Lapu-Lapu City), Inc. provides products and services including lending (business and consumer) and deposit-taking.

Through its ten (10) branches strategically located throughout the region, it can accommodate a wide base of clientele in the Eastern and Central Visayas Region. The bank’s focus is to bring its products and services to the countryside and fulfill its mandate as a rural bank.

How we started

Three decades of steady growth.

From one rural bank in Mactan to ten branches serving Central and Eastern Visayas.

  1. 1994

    The beginning

    Mactanbank secured approval from the Central Bank of the Philippines through Resolution No. 506, authorizing it to operate as a rural bank.

  2. 1998

    New management

    The previous stockholders sold their entire holdings to the Yu Family. The new management was headed by the family of the late Mr. Ernesto “Asay” Yu.

  3. 2008

    A better home

    With BSP approval, the bank transferred from Basak-Iba to MYFC Bldg., Patalinghug Avenue, Pajo, Lapu-Lapu City.

  4. 2010+

    Branch expansion

    The bank expanded north and south of Cebu, then across the Visayas with branches in Tacloban, Ormoc and Dumaguete.

  5. 2019

    25 years

    Mactan Bank celebrated its 25th anniversary of serving local communities.

  6. 2023

    30 years

    Three decades of service — operating across Regions 7 and 8 and the Negros Island Region.
