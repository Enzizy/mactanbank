How to open

Open a time deposit in two steps.

1

Go to your branch of choice

Visit any of our ten Mactan Bank branches across Cebu, Leyte and Negros Oriental.

2

Bring 2 valid IDs and your initial deposit

Our team will open your account and hand you your passbook.

Your deposit is protected.Deposits are insured by PDIC up to ₱1 Million per depositor.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions.

What is the minimum placement?

The minimum placement is ₱10,000.00.

Is the interest taxed?

Yes. Interest is subject to 20% withholding tax.

Can I borrow against my time deposit?

Yes — with a Back-to-Back Loan you can borrow up to 90% of your deposit.

Where can I open a savings account?

Please go to the nearest Mactan Bank branch office.
