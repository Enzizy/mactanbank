Loans

What are the requirements for business and agricultural loans?

Corporate

  • Duly registered with the appropriate government regulating body.
  • For borrowers applying for ₱500,000 and below, the corporation/institution should be operating for at least one (1) year prior to loan application for the same business applied for.
  • For borrowers applying for ₱500,001 and above, the corporation/institution should be operating for at least two (2) years prior to loan application for the same business applied for.
  • No adverse news on the industry.
  • Without social resistance in the area of operation.
  • Good credit standing with other banks & financial institutions.

Individual

  • The borrower must be 18–70 years old at the time the loan is granted.
  • Must have no pending criminal cases.
  • Good credit standing with other banks & other financial institutions.
How long does it take to get a loan?

We can guarantee a maximum of 30–40 minute processing time.

Are there any hidden charges?

We assure you that there are none.

Do I need to open a savings account with you to get a loan?

No, there is no need.

Where should I go?

Please go to the nearest Mactan Bank branch office.

Teacher’s salary loan

What are the requirements?

For a new loan

  • Two (2) months latest payslips (current and previous month, original and photocopy)
  • Duly accomplished Loan Application Form
  • Three (3) photocopies of DepEd and PRC IDs
  • Certified true copy of the Permanent Appointment (back to back)

For a reloan

  • Two (2) months latest payslips (current and previous month, original and photocopy)
  • Duly accomplished Salary Loan Application Form
  • Two (2) photocopies of DepEd and PRC IDs (back to back)

For more information, please call your nearest branch.

What is the new loan application process?

The steps under the new DepEd Terms and Conditions of the APDS Accreditation (TCAA):

  1. Visit a branch. Bring your loan documents and payslips so we can evaluate your loan eligibility.
  2. Email your DepEd verifier. Using your DepEd-issued email address, send your evaluated loan application details and stamped payslip to your DepEd Verifier.
  3. DepEd confirms. Your DepEd Verifier approves and sends a confirmation via email to Mactanbank, cc: the teacher.
  4. Sign and receive. Visit your Mactanbank branch to sign the loan documents and immediately receive your loan proceeds.
How long is the new loan application process?
  • Steps 1 & 2 can be completed within an hour of your visit to any of our branches.
  • Step 3 depends on the DepEd Verifier’s load and there is no committed turnaround time.
  • The last step can be completed within an hour after we receive the confirmation from the DepEd Verifier and you have signed all the loan documents in the branch.
Do you accept redemptions?

Yes, depending on the evaluation of your payslip. For more inquiries, please visit or call your nearest branch.

How much is the minimum placement for a time deposit, and what is its interest rate?

The minimum placement is ₱10,000.00. See our time deposit rates.

When will an account be considered dormant?

After 2 years of no deposit or withdrawal activity, an account can be considered dormant.

What do I do if I lose my passbook?

You must immediately submit an affidavit of loss. A fee of ₱150.00 will be charged for the replacement of the passbook.
