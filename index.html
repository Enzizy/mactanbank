How it works

Getting started is simple.

Every loan and savings product is available in all Mactan Bank offices.

1

Choose your product

Compare our loans and deposits online, or call your nearest branch for help choosing.

2

Visit a branch

Bring two valid IDs. For loans, bring the requirements listed on each product page.

3

Get it done

Loans are processed in a maximum of 30–40 minutes. Savings accounts come with your own passbook.
