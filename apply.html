Data Privacy Notice

Mactan Bank, Inc. (“the Bank”) is committed to the proper handling and safeguarding of your personal data. This notice explains how the Bank collects your personal data through this website and how we process it in compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012.

We collect only what allows us to respond to your application and initially validate your identity: loan details, personal and spouse information, employment information, existing obligations, pending applications and authority-to-credit details.

Records are kept for five (5) years and then destroyed, unless a longer period is required by law or regulators. Read the full Data Privacy Policy.
